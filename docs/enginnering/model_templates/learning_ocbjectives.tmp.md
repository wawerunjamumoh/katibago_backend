===================================================

Entity

LearningObjective

===================================================

Purpose

Defines a measurable learning outcome that a learner
should achieve after completing an Article.

---

Questions it answers

What should the learner understand?

What should the learner identify?

What should the learner be able to explain?

What mistake should they stop making?

What constitutional principle should they remember?

---

Responsibilities

Describe learning outcomes.

Guide Case selection.

Guide Challenge creation.

Measure lesson completion.

Business Rules

Every Learning Objective belongs to one Article.

Each Article must contain at least two Learning Objectives.

Objectives should be measurable.

Objectives should not describe implementation.
