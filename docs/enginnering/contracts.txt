Chapter Contract (v1)
Purpose
A Chapter is the highest-level learning container in KatibaGo.

It groups related constitutional Articles into a logical learning journey.

It provides organization for learners but does not directly deliver learning activities.

Responsibilities
A Chapter is responsible for:

Organizing Articles into a constitutional topic.

Providing the learner's first level of navigation.

Displaying introductory information about the topic.

Defining whether the Chapter is available to learners.

Providing metadata for curriculum organization.

Does NOT Own
A Chapter does not own:

Learning Objectives

Cases

Decision Points

Choices

User Progress

Citizen Explanations

Constitution Text

It only owns the relationship to Articles.

Identity
A Chapter is uniquely identified by:

Business Rules
Chapter numbers must be unique.

Chapter numbers must match the Constitution.

Every Article belongs to exactly one Chapter.

A Chapter may temporarily contain zero Articles while content is being developed.

Inactive Chapters cannot be accessed by learners.

Future Extensions
Possible additions without changing the model's purpose:

Cover image

Theme color

Estimated completion time

XP available in Chapter

Completion badge

Article Contract (v1)
Purpose
An Article is the smallest learner-facing study unit.

It represents one constitutional Article transformed into an interactive lesson.

Responsibilities
An Article is responsible for:

Delivering one learning session.

Selecting the Learning Objectives.

Selecting reusable Cases.

Providing progression metadata.

Connecting learners to the official constitutional content.

Does NOT Own
An Article does not own:

Cases

Learning Objectives

Decision Points

Choices

Safety Shields

Citizen Explanations

Official Constitution Text

User Progress

It owns only the relationships to those entities.

Business Rules
Article numbers are unique.

Every Article belongs to one Chapter.

Every Article should have at least one Learning Objective before publication.

Every published Article should reference at least one Case.

XP cannot be negative.

Estimated duration must be positive.

Future Extensions
Without changing the model's purpose, we can later support:

Voice narration

Multiple constitutional editions
